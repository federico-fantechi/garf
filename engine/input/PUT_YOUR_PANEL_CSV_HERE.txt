Put your panel CSV in THIS folder.

By default config.yaml looks for:   input/panel.csv
(so the simplest option is to name your file panel.csv and drop it here).

If your file has a different name, either rename it to panel.csv, or edit the
line  data_path: input/panel.csv  in ../config.yaml to match your file name.

The CSV must be a long-format panel: one row per unit-year, with at least the
columns named in config.yaml (id, year, treatment 0/1, outcome, and covariates).
You can delete this note.
